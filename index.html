✨ What Is x-ambient?

x-ambient is a small companion app for your desktop that changes how your X timeline feels. Normally, scrolling past a photo gives you nothing but a picture in a box. With x-ambient running, the moment your mouse pointer rests on a photo or a video, the colors inside that picture spill out and fill the entire page around it.

A sunset turns your whole window warm orange. A night city shot washes everything in deep blue. A bright product photo brightens the page like a soft lamp. When you move your pointer away, the light gently fades and everything returns to normal.

There is nothing to configure if you do not want to. You install it, you open it, and you browse. The glow does the rest.

🛠️ Troubleshooting

The page is not glowing at all

Close x-ambient completely and open it again. Wait a few seconds after it starts, then move your pointer slowly over a large photo on your timeline. Also make sure the browser window you are browsing in is the window currently in front of you.

The glow appears, but it is very faint

Faint light usually means the photo you are hovering over is itself light or low-contrast. Try a photo with strong, deep colors such as a sunset or a night scene, and check whether your screen brightness is turned down very low.

The glow feels delayed

Close other tabs and heavy programs you are not using. The effect reacts faster when your computer has a little spare breathing room.

The download did not start

Return to the release page using the download button on this page and try again. Some browsers block automatic downloads until you confirm them, so watch for a small prompt near the top of your browser window.

I want it to stop for a while

Simply close the x-ambient window. The ambient light stops immediately and your page returns to its normal look. You can start it again whenever you like.
